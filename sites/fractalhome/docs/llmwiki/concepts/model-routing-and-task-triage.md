Route bounded, reversible, and deterministically verifiable work to an economical throughput lane; route familiar but uncertain work to a general lane; and reserve premium reasoning for unresolved ambiguity or changes with costly failure. Make the policy observable through structured evidence, targeted checks, escalation triggers, execution-trajectory signals, and measurements of retries, review burden, and escaped defects.
