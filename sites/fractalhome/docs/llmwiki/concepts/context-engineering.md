Context is a limited attention resource. Prefer clear instructions, narrow tools, just-in-time retrieval, compaction or resets, persistent notes, and focused workers. Durable decision records benefit from policy versions, evidence, and outcome links so later retrieval can distinguish usable precedent from merely similar text.
