Context graphs add decision records, evidence, exceptions, and versioned policy links to entity-and-relationship graphs. Their key property is an append-only write path: a resolved case becomes retrievable precedent for later, comparable work. Reliability depends on strong identity resolution, policy versioning, qualified retrieval, and human escalation for ambiguous matches.
