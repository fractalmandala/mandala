An AI-native operating model places people at the goal-setting and quality-judgment boundaries while agents execute within defined skills, tools, and context. Its memory loop captures events, curates and stores them, uses them during execution, and feeds real-world outcomes back into the system. Durable traces and QA are necessary for the organization to learn rather than merely automate.
