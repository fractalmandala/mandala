Graph engineering models a workflow as bounded jobs joined only by real data dependencies. Parallelism belongs where sibling work is independent; barriers belong where a stage requires the whole set. A reliable graph also defines validated node contracts, deterministic transforms, verifier edges, isolated writers, convergence rules, model tiers, and caps on rounds and population.
