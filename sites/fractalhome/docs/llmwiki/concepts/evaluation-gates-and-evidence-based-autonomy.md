An evaluation gate turns evidence into a run-time control: it may block a handoff, quarantine a branch, require escalation, revoke certification, or prevent release. Prefer deterministic proof for objective claims, independently grounded judges for subjective criteria, trajectory checks alongside outcome checks, and blast-radius-based autonomy lanes. Treat automated confidence as one weak signal; retain a human gate for costly or irreversible actions.
