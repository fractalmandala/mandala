This knowledge bank covers the collected works of **Sita Ram Goel** and **Ram Swarup**, two of the most prominent Hindu intellectual voices of post-independence India, published primarily through **Voice of India**, New Delhi. Their writings span sustained critiques of Islam, Christianity, communism, and Nehruvian secularism, alongside affirmations of Sanatana Dharma, Hindu society, and Indian cultural nationalism.

## Topic Map

| Topic | Description |
|---|---|
| [Defence of Hindu Society](/wiki/04-srg-srs/defence-of-hindu-society) | Sita Ram Goel's comprehensive diagnosis of threats to Hindu society from monotheistic creeds, leftist ideologies, and pseudo-secularism |
| [Hindu Temples and Islamic Iconoclasm](/wiki/04-srg-srs/hindu-temples-and-islamic-iconoclasm) | Two-volume documentation of mass destruction of Hindu temples by Muslim rulers, with theological and historical evidence |
| [Islamic Imperialism in India](/wiki/04-srg-srs/islamic-imperialism-in-india) | Analysis of the pattern of Muslim rule as continuous imperialist aggression against Hindu society and culture |
| [Critical Study of Islam](/wiki/04-srg-srs/critical-study-of-islam) | Examination of Islamic doctrine through Hadis, Quranic analysis, and the doctrine of jihad |
| [Woman in Islam](/wiki/04-srg-srs/woman-in-islam) | Ram Swarup's study of Islamic laws on marriage, divorce, polygamy, and women's status |
| [Hindu-Christian Encounters](/wiki/04-srg-srs/hindu-christian-encounters) | Chronological survey of Hindu responses to Christian missionary aggression from AD 304 to 1996 |
| [Christian Missions and Papacy](/wiki/04-srg-srs/christian-missions-and-papacy) | Sita Ram Goel's exposé of the Vatican's empire in India, the Niyogi Committee Report, and missionary indigenisation tactics |
| [Jesus Christ: Critical Analysis](/wiki/04-srg-srs/jesus-christ-critical-analysis) | Historical and theological critique of Jesus as an artifice for Christian imperialist aggression |
| [Hindu View of Christianity and Islam](/wiki/04-srg-srs/hindu-view-of-christianity-and-islam) | Ram Swarup's comparative study contrasting dharmic traditions with monotheistic creeds |
| [India's Secularism](/wiki/04-srg-srs/indias-secularism) | Critique of the concept of secularism as a tool to weaken Hindu society |
| [Sikhism and Hindu-Sikh Unity](/wiki/04-srg-srs/sikhism-and-hindu-sikh-unity) | Ram Swarup's exposition of Sikhism's roots in Hinduism and the historical unity of Hindus and Sikhs |
| [Critique of Communism](/wiki/04-srg-srs/critique-of-communism) | Analysis of Maoism, communist state terror, and the serial liquidation of communist leaders |
| [Gandhian Philosophy and Economics](/wiki/04-srg-srs/gandhian-philosophy-and-economics) | Ram Swarup's examination of Gandhism as a principled alternative to communism |
| [Hindu View of Education](/wiki/04-srg-srs/hindu-view-of-education) | Ram Swarup's speech on education rooted in India's spiritual traditions |
| [Muslim Separatism](/wiki/04-srg-srs/muslim-separatism) | Analysis of the causes, ideological roots, and continuing consequences of Muslim separatism in India |
| [Theory and Practice of Muslim State](/wiki/04-srg-srs/theory-practice-of-muslim-state) | K. S. Lal's study tracing Muslim governance in India to Quranic and Hadithic injunctions |
| [Perversion of Political Parlance](/wiki/04-srg-srs/perversion-of-political-parlance) | Sita Ram Goel's diagnosis of how leftist language distorts Indian political discourse |
| [Freedom of Expression](/wiki/04-srg-srs/freedom-of-expression) | Documents the legal and political struggles over free speech on Islam and Christianity in India |
| [On Hinduism: Reviews and Reflections](/wiki/04-srg-srs/on-hinduism-reviews-and-reflections) | Ram Swarup's wide-ranging reflections on Hindu dharma and its global significance |
| [Word as Revelation](/wiki/04-srg-srs/word-as-revelation) | Ram Swarup's study of the names of God and the concept of revelation across traditions |
| [An Indictment of 1942 Leadership](/wiki/04-srg-srs/indictment-of-1942-leadership) | Ram Swarup's early pamphlet on the failure of leadership in the 1942 Quit India movement |
