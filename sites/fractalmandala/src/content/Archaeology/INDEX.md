The archaeology knowledge bank covers the **Indian Journal of Archaeology** (ISSN: applied), a peer-reviewed e-journal published from 2016 to 2026 under the chief editorship of Vijay Kumar. The journal documents antiquities, excavations, explorations, museum collections, and research across the Indian subcontinent, spanning from the Lower Palaeolithic to the modern period.

## Topic Map

- [Indian Journal of Archaeology (Journal Overview)](/wiki/07-archaeology/indian-journal-of-archaeology) — The journal's scope, editors, and complete issue listing across 40 volumes
- [Copper Hoard and OCP Culture](/wiki/07-archaeology/copper-hoard-ocp-culture) — Ochre Coloured Pottery (OCP) and Copper Hoard antiquities from the Gangetic plain
- [Rock Art of India](/wiki/07-archaeology/rock-art-india) — Painted rock shelters and petroglyphs from Chitrakoot, Malaprabha, Likhichhaj, Sonbhadra, and beyond
- [Palaeoclimate and Quaternary Studies](/wiki/07-archaeology/palaeoclimate-quaternary) — Pollen-based vegetation and climate reconstruction from lake sediments across India and the Himalaya
- [Palaeolithic and Microlithic Archaeology](/wiki/07-archaeology/palaeolithic-microlithic) — Acheulian, Soanian, and microlithic industries from the Vindhyas, Odisha, Siwaliks, and other regions
- [Harappan and Indus Civilization](/wiki/07-archaeology/harappan-indus) — Excavations at Khanak, Sampolia Khera, Kanmer, Alamgirpur, Rakhigarhi, and related studies
- [Excavations in the Gangetic Plain](/wiki/07-archaeology/excavations-gangetic-plain) — Key excavations at Hetapatti, Naimisharanya, Agiabir, Barood Khera, Nindaur, and other sites
- [Numismatics](/wiki/07-archaeology/numismatics) — Coin hoards, punch-marked coins, and numismatic studies from the Mauryan to Mughal periods
- [Seals and Sealings](/wiki/07-archaeology/seals-and-sealings) — Catalogues of seals and sealings from museums across India
- [Temple Architecture and Sculpture](/wiki/07-archaeology/temple-architecture) — Temple architecture, iconography, and sculptural traditions across India
- [Medieval and Mughal Archaeology](/wiki/07-archaeology/medieval-mughal) — Tombs, mosques, forts, gardens, and waterworks of the Sultanate and Mughal periods
- [Museum Collection Catalogues](/wiki/07-archaeology/museum-catalogues) — Systematic catalogues of antiquities from State Museum Lucknow, Kannauj Museum, Mathura Museum, and others
- [Archaeological Gazetteers](/wiki/07-archaeology/archaeological-gazetteers) — District-wise gazetteers documenting archaeological sites in Sitapur, Aligarh, Kannauj, and Maharajganj
- [Inscriptions and Epigraphy](/wiki/07-archaeology/inscriptions-epigraphy) — Newly discovered inscriptions from Kalinjar, Kannauj, Kaimur, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, and Persian epigraphs
- [Megaliths and Ethnoarchaeology](/wiki/07-archaeology/megaliths-ethnoarchaeology) — Megalithic burials, living megalithic traditions, and ethnoarchaeological studies
- [Jaina and Buddhist Archaeology](/wiki/07-archaeology/jaina-buddhist) — Jaina art and iconography, Buddhist sites, sculptures, and sealing traditions
- [Archaeobotany and Archaeozoology](/wiki/07-archaeology/archaeobotany-archaeozoology) — Plant remains, faunal analysis, and subsistence strategies in ancient India
- [Archaeometry and Genetics](/wiki/07-archaeology/archaeometry-genetics) — Scientific techniques including FT-IR spectroscopy, radiocarbon dating, pollen morphology, and Y-haplogroup studies
- [Heritage Management and Conservation](/wiki/07-archaeology/heritage-conservation) — Conservation, virtual archaeology, site museums, and heritage politics
- [Regional Archaeology: Odisha](/wiki/07-archaeology/regional-odisha) — Prehistoric, protohistoric, and early historic sites in Odisha
- [Regional Archaeology: Kashmir and Himalayas](/wiki/07-archaeology/regional-kashmir) — Neolithic sites, temple architecture, and settlement patterns in Kashmir and the Himalayan region
