The journal publishes extensive numismatic research, documenting coin hoards and museum collections from the punch-marked period through the Mughal era.

## Major Coin Studies

### Punch-Marked Coins
- **Imaliya Sultanpur, Sitapur** (VOL-3-04): 558 silver punch-marked coins of Magadha type
- **Shravasti, Uttar Pradesh** (VOL-2-04): Kośala Mahajanapada type early punch-marked coins
- **Jajmau, Kanpur** (VOL-6-01): Imperial Magadha punch-marked coins
- **Alwar, Rajasthan** (VOL-9-01): Hoard of punch-marked coins
- **Gonda, Uttar Pradesh** (VOL-9-01): Hoard of punch-marked coins
- **Kaushambi** (VOL-9-04): Hoard of uninscribed copper coins

### Regional Kingdoms
- **Panchal Kings** (VOL-9-04): Coins of Panchal kings including King Achyut, from the Shahjad Rai Research Institute collection
- **Panchal Kings — Sanjay Agarwal Collection** (VOL-10-01): Coins of Panchal kings from Bareilly
- **Koch Bihar** (VOL-2-04): Nārāyanī coins of the Koch Bihar kingdom

### Kushan, Gupta, and Later
- **Kushan coins from Rataul, Baghpat** (VOL-9-01): Hoard of Kushan coins
- **Indo-Greek, Kushan & Gupta gold coins** (VOL-7-04): State Museum Lucknow collection
- **Medieval & South Indian gold coins** (VOL-8-01): State Museum Lucknow
- **Gold coins of different periods** (VOL-9-02): Allahabad Museum collection
- **Delhi Sultanate, Mughal, Awadh gold coins** (VOL-8-02): State Museum Lucknow catalogue
- **Sultanate coins from Gangoh, Saharanpur** (VOL-9-01): Hoard of Sultanate coins
- **Alauddin Khilji coin hoard from Khedi Khushnam, Shamli** (VOL-6-04)

### Didda of Kashmir (VOL-4-01)
Prashant Srivastava and Pratichi Srivastava reconstructed events from the long reign of Queen Didda of Kashmir through numismatic evidence and the legend "Di-Kshemagupta" on coins.

### Sitapur and Banda Coins (VOL-1-02)
Yashwant Singh Rathore documented coins from different sites of districts Sitapur and Banda, Uttar Pradesh.
