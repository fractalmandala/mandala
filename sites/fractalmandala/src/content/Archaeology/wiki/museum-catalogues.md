The journal has published extensive catalogues of museum collections from across India, systematically documenting sculptures, terracottas, coins, and other antiquities. These catalogues form an important resource for scholars studying Indian art and archaeology.

## State Museum, Lucknow

### Terracottas
- **Part I: Pre-Mauryan to Kushan Period** (VOL-5-03): Catalogue of terracottas including Mauryan images of Vasudhara and Mansa, Shunga images of Gajalakshmi and proto-Durga, and Kushan images of Kuber, Hariti, and Naigamesha.
- **Part II: Gupta Period to Modern Period** (VOL-5-04): A single-article issue with extensive catalogue from Gupta to modern terracottas, including ethno-archaeological study of votive offerings to guardian deities.

### Sculptures
- **Part II: 3rd century BCE to 9th-10th century CE** (VOL-5-01): Stone sculptures from the State Museum.
- **Part III: 10th century CE to Modern Period** (VOL-5-02): Later sculptures catalogue.

### Coins
- **Gold Coins: Delhi Sultanate, Mughal, Awadh, British & Modern** (VOL-8-02): Gold coins catalogue.
- **Indo-Greek, Kushan & Gupta Gold Coins** (VOL-7-04): Earlier gold coin collection.
- **Medieval & South Indian Gold Coins** (VOL-8-01): Later period gold coins.

## Government Archaeological Museum, Kannauj

- **Part I** (VOL-3-01): Terracotta images and miscellaneous objects from Kannauj city, tracing Indian culture from Iron Age to early medieval period.
- **Part II** (VOL-3-02): Stone images including door jambs, chandrashala motifs, Kannauj sculptures, and Buddhist sculptures.

## Government Museum, Mathura

- **Terracotta Antiquities** (VOL-8-04): Terracotta collection from Mathura.
- **Stone Sculptures** (VOL-9-01): Stone sculpture catalogue.

## Allahabad Museum, Prayagraj

- **Seals and Sealings** (VOL-6-03): Comprehensive single-issue catalogue (see [Seals and Sealings](/wiki/07-archaeology/seals-and-sealings)).
- **Gold Coins of Different Periods** (VOL-9-02): Gold coin catalogue.

## Other Museum Collections

- **Buddha Museum, Gorakhpur** (VOL-4-01): Terracottas and sculptures from 2nd century BCE to 6th century CE.
- **Ram Katha Sangrahalaya and Kaushal Museum, Ayodhya** (VOL-4-01): Sculptures, terracottas, and other antiquities.
- **Aman Singh Palace Museum, Kalinjar Fort** (VOL-4-03): Antiquities from Kalinjar fort, Banda.
- **Shahjad Rai Research Institute, Baraut** (VOL-9-03): Catalogue of terracotta objects.
- **Pandit Surendra Mohan Mishra Museum, Chandausi** (VOL-7-03): Terracotta catalogue.
- **Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Roohelkhand University Museum, Bareilly** (VOL-8-03): Terracotta collection.
- **Lucknow Museum** (VOL-2-03): Terracottas by Amar Singh and Yashwant Singh Rathore.
- **State Archaeological Museum, Lucknow — Jain, Bauddha & Gandhar Antiquities** (VOL-4-04).
