The corpus contains several sources that do not fit neatly into the major topic categories. This page provides an overview of these miscellaneous works.

## Travel and Cultural Studies

- **Modern Traveller (Vols. 1–3)**: Travel accounts describing places, cultures, and historical sites
- **Roots**: General study on cultural and historical roots

## Social and Political Commentary

- **A Review of Beef in India**: A study of beef consumption and its cultural history in India
- **Truth About Gandhi**: Critical examination of Mahatma Gandhi's life and legacy
- **How I Became a Hindu** (Sita Ram Goel): Personal and intellectual journey into Hinduism, critiquing both colonial and Marxist historiography
- **FREE BY NATURE**: Collected blog writings on cultural and political topics

## Religious and Philosophical

- **Ram Krishna**: Life and teachings of Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa
- **The Riks P. Aiyar**: Philosophical commentary on the Rig Veda
- **Sanskrit Svayam Shikshak**: Self-teaching manuals for Sanskrit
- **Xavier Rouard**: Work on comparative religion and mythology

## Miscellaneous Scholarly Works

- **Klaus Schmidt Memoriam**: Tribute to the archaeologist of Göbekli Tepe
- **Cavalli**: On the work of Luigi Luca Cavalli-Sforza in population genetics
- **The Wild Tribes of India**: Ethnographic survey of tribal communities
- **Skeletal Record** (Kenneth Kennedy): Bio-anthropological study of ancient Indian skeletal remains
- **Study of Example-Based English to Sanskrit Machine Translation**: Linguistic/technological work
