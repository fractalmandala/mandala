The corpus contains a substantial collection of reference works, including indexes of Vedic terms, Sanskrit dictionaries, and compilations of Vedic mythology.

## Vedic Indexes

- **Vedic Main Index**: Comprehensive index of Vedic terms and their occurrences
- **Vedic Index 1 & 2**: Additional indexing of key terms and concepts
- **Vedic Terms Vol. 1 & 2**: Detailed entries for technical Vedic vocabulary
- **Vedic Mythology Index**: Deities, mythical figures, and related terms

## Sanskrit Dictionaries

- **Sanskrit Dictionary 0 & 1**: Lexical resources for Sanskrit vocabulary
- Covering both Vedic and Classical Sanskrit

## Usage

These reference works serve as tools for:
- Locating specific terms in Vedic texts
- Understanding semantic ranges of key concepts
- Cross-referencing mythological figures and epithets
- Identifying etymological connections

## Related Resources in the Corpus

- Vedic Grammar (grammatical analysis)
- Terminology of the Vedas (glossary of key terms)
- Study of Example-Based English to Sanskrit Machine Translation
