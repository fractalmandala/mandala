## Blockchain and Cryptocurrency

[Understanding the Philosophy of Blockchain and Cryptocurrency](/writings/blog/cryptoprimer) (2017) explains the ideological and political dimensions of blockchain technology. The core concept is **decentralization** — enabling human interactions without third-party intermediaries. The essay traces this from Bitcoin's challenge to central banking through the broader implications for human coordination, arguing that blockchain may be the most revolutionary idea in human history.
