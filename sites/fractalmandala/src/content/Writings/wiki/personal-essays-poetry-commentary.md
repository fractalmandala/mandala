The personal essays, poetry, and commentary writings form the most diverse category, ranging from introductions and [about pages](/writings/blog/about) to experimental poetry, cultural observations, and reflections on writing and creativity.

## About and Introduction

[About Fractal Maṇḍala](/writings/blog/about) introduces Amrit as a writer and creator based in Gurgaon, India, with a background in management and product development, currently working as Director of Strategy at Bṛhat. The Fractal Maṇḍala is described as a digital garden to chronicle learning and building — a space for web products, historical fiction, psytrance production, and essays on Indian history and civilizational consciousness.

## Poetry

[The Unpoems](/writings/blog/unpoems) is a collection of poetry exploring truth, lies, and the ineffable. The poems are spare, direct, and emotionally charged — refusing ornamentation in favor of raw experiential truth. The opening poem "truth" captures the visceral confrontation with an unwanted truth that cannot be undone.
