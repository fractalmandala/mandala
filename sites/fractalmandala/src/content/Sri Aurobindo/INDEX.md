This knowledge bank covers the **Complete Works of Sri Aurobindo (CWSA)** across 18 volumes published by the Sri Aurobindo Ashram Trust. Sri Aurobindo (1872–1950) was an Indian philosopher, yogi, poet, and nationalist whose writings span spiritual philosophy, yoga, Vedic and Upanishadic interpretation, political theory, social philosophy, literary criticism, education, and art. The corpus includes his magnum opus *The Life Divine*, the integral yoga system of *The Synthesis of Yoga*, commentaries on the Bhagavad Gita and the Upanishads, psychological interpretation of the Vedas, nationalist political writings, and extensive correspondence with disciples.
