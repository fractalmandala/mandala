Odisha features prominently in the journal's publications, with studies covering prehistoric microlithic settlements, early historic fortified sites, and megalithic traditions.

## Prehistoric Sites

### Burla, Sambalpur (VOL-2-03)
Excavations revealed microliths and pebble-cobble tools below tephra, indicating a late Pleistocene chronology. The site is interpreted as a factory-cum-base camp.

### Tang River Valley, Bolangir (VOL-5-01)
Five microlithic settlements (Siletpada, Jhinkidunguri, Mahagaon, Bhoipada) with 854 lithic components. Chert of 12 varieties was the main raw material.

### Middle Brahmani Valley (VOL-9-01)
Examining raw material variability and tool production strategies at prehistoric sites.

### Khadga River Valley (VOL-8-02)
Newly discovered prehistoric sites in the Khadga valley.

### Lower Khadga Stream, Kandhmal (VOL-7-02)
Preliminary investigations in this stream valley.

### Sundar River Valley (VOL-5-01)
Early Historic culture documented across 11 sites in the Sundar river valley.

### Dulung River Valley (VOL-6-04)
Located in West Bengal but part of the Subarnarekha basin, 28 sites yielded Palaeolithic and microlithic implements.

### Rock Shelters of Bargarh (VOL-4-03)
Recently explored prehistoric rock shelters in Bargarh district.

## Protohistoric and Early Historic

### Subulia, Middle Mahanadi Valley (VOL-2-04)
A Late Chalcolithic settlement in the Middle Mahanadi Valley, by Pradeep K. Behera and Sakir Hussain.

### Circular Fortified Settlements (VOL-4-02)
Sakir Hussain's preliminary observation on circular fortified settlements in the Middle Mahanadi Valley.

### Jamsaragarh, Sundargarh (VOL-5-01)
A note on an early historic fortified settlement yielding a variety of pottery pieces.

### Radhanagar (VOL-8-01)
An early historic urban settlement of Odisha.

### Parabhadi (Sukhuapada), Jajpur (VOL-8-03)
Excavation report of this Odishan site by Dibishada Brajasundar Garnayak et al.

### Barpali Asurgarh (VOL-10-04)
Preliminary report on the excavation of this early historical fortified settlement.

### Budhigarh Excavation, Kalahandi (VOL-7-03)
Typology and chronology of pottery in the Tel River valley region: a case study of the Budhigarh excavation.

## Ethnoarchaeology

### Megalithic Tradition among Munda, Bonaigarh (VOL-4-01)
Living megalithic tradition among the Munda community of Sundergarh.

### Potters of Kuhibahal, Subarnapur (VOL-7-02)
Study of traditional potters continuing ancient techniques.
