Kashmir and the Himalayan region are covered extensively in the journal, from Neolithic sites to medieval temple architecture.

## Neolithic Kashmir

### Settlement Pattern and Material Culture (VOL-7-04)
Abdul Adil Paray and Manoj Kumar's study of the settlement pattern and material culture of Neolithic sites in the Kashmir Valley.

### Burzahom (VOL-10-02)
Echoes of Neolithic culture in the heart of the Himalayas: a revisit to this key Neolithic site.

### FT-IR Analysis of Neolithic Pottery (VOL-9-04)
Spectroscopic analysis of pottery from Gufkral, Hariparigam, Pethpuranteng, and Qasimbagh, revealing four diagnostic categories: coarse grey-ware, burnished grey-ware, gritty dull red-ware, and fine grey-ware.

### Kanispur Fauna (VOL-4-03)
Animal remains from Kanispur, district Baramulla.

## Temple Architecture in Kashmir

### Ancient Hindu Temple Architecture of South Kashmir (VOL-3-04)
Arif Ahmad Dar and Sandeep Kumar Pandey describe how South Kashmir's temple architecture was influenced by Gandhara, Kushan, and Greco-Roman styles.

### Unique Features of Ancient Hindu Temple Architecture of Kashmir (VOL-7-03)
A further study of distinctive characteristics of Kashmiri temple architecture.

### Sufi Architecture in Kashmir (VOL-10-02)
The influence of Sufism on the built heritage of Kashmir: architectural evolution and religious syncretism.

### Memorial Stelae from Lolab Valley, Kupwara (VOL-8-03)
Typology and iconography of memorial stelae from the Lolab valley.

## Settlement and Survey

### Archaeological Survey of Kashmir Tehsils (VOL-7-02)
Abdul Adil Paray's survey of Awantipora (Pulwama), Narvaw (Baramulla), and Ahan (Ganderbal).

### Ichnad Village, Aishmuqam, Anantnag (VOL-8-03)
Unearthing mysteries: an archaeological survey of Ichnad village.

### Politics of Heritage in Kashmir (1846-1947) (VOL-2-03)
Abdul Rashid Lone's study of the state of archaeology in Kashmir under Dogra rule.

### Socio-Economic History of Ancient Kashmir (VOL-11-01)
Irfan Bashir and Abdul Rashid Lone on religious influence and socioeconomic dynamics in ancient and early medieval Kashmir.

## Himalayan Prehistory

### Siwalik Frontal Range (VOL-7-04, VOL-9-02)
Core and flake assemblages from Ghumarwin (Bilaspur) and Kasohal in the Siwalik frontal range of Himachal Pradesh.

### Brahmaputra Valley Prehistory (VOL-7-03)
Historical and archaeological retracking of the prehistoric landscape of the Brahmaputra valley.

### Rock Structures in Himalayan Highlands (VOL-8-01)
Possible comparison with megaliths: an ethnographic analysis.

### Pastoralism in Northwest Himalayas (VOL-6-04)
An ethno-archaeological perspective.
