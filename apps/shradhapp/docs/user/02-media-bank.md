# The Media Bank

The Media Bank is your permanent library. Everything you import stays here, neatly
organized, ready to use in any video you make — today or next year.

## Adding things to the bank

There are two easy ways:

**Way 1 — the button**

1. Click **➕ Add videos, photos or audio** at the top of the Media Bank tab.
2. A file-picker window opens. Click the files you want (hold Ctrl or Cmd to pick
   several at once), then confirm.
3. The files are copied into the bank and appear in the grid.

**Way 2 — drag and drop**

1. Find your files in Finder (Mac) or File Explorer (Windows).
2. Drag them onto the Shradhapp window.
3. While you drag, the window shows a dashed orange frame saying
   *"Drop files to add them to your Media Bank"*. Let go, and the files are imported.

> **Good to know:** importing makes a *copy* inside the app. Your original file is
> never moved, renamed or changed.

**What you can import:**

- Videos: mp4, mov, mkv, avi, webm, m4v, mpg, mpeg
- Photos: png, jpg, jpeg, gif, bmp, webp, heic
- Audio: mp3, wav, m4a, aac, ogg, flac, opus

If a file isn't one of these, the app tells you politely that it doesn't understand it.

## What you'll see in the grid

Each item appears as a card with:

- A **picture preview** — for videos a frame from the clip, for photos the photo
  itself, and for audio a drawn **waveform** (the shape of the sound, so you can
  recognize recordings at a glance).
- A small **label** saying *Video*, *Photo* or *Audio*.
- The **length** of the clip (e.g. `1:15`) for videos and audio.
- The **name**, and any **tags** you've added (shown as `#tag`).

## Finding things

At the top of the tab you have three helpers:

1. **Search box** — type anything that appears in the name, tags or notes of an item.
   For example, typing `birthday` finds the file, the tag, or a note mentioning it.
2. **Kind buttons** — click **Everything**, **🎬 Videos**, **🖼️ Photos** or
   **🎵 Audio** to show just that kind.
3. **Tag menu** — once you've tagged things, a menu appears (starting with
   *"All tags"*) where you can pick one tag, like `#family`, to see only those items.

## The detail panel

Click any card to open its detail panel on the right. Here's what you can do:

- **Preview it.** Videos play with a normal video player, photos show large, and audio
  shows its waveform with a play button underneath.
- **See its facts.** Length, picture size (e.g. 1920×1080), and the date it was added.
- **Rename it.** Type a new name in the *Name* box and click **Rename** (or press
  Enter). Names can't be empty.
- **Add tags.** Type a word in the tag box and press Enter — it becomes a chip like
  `#garden`. Tags are tidied automatically: capitals become lowercase and a leading
  `#` is removed if you typed one. Click the ✕ on a chip to remove that tag.
- **Write notes.** A bigger box for anything you want to remember — "film again with
  better light", "use for Lily's video", and so on. Notes are saved when you click
  outside the box.
- **Remove it.** Click **Remove from bank**, then confirm with **Yes, remove it**
  (or change your mind with **Keep it**). Removing deletes the app's *copy* of the
  file — your original file wherever it lives is untouched.

Click the ✕ at the top of the panel to close it.

## Tips

- Tag things as you import them — a few words like `family`, `garden`, `holiday`
  make the bank effortless to search later.
- Recordings you make in the [Record Voiceover](./03-recording-voiceovers.md) tab are
  saved here automatically with the tag `#voiceover`.
- Items in the bank are what you assemble from in
  [Make a Video](./04-making-a-video.md).
