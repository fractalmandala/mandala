# Making a video

The **🎬 Make a Video** tab is where your video comes together. On the left is a list
of your video projects; on the right is the editor where you arrange clips.

Everything here **saves itself automatically** — a little *"✓ Saved"* appears beside
the title whenever a change is stored. There's no Save button to remember.

## Starting a new video

1. In the left column, click **＋ New**.
2. Type a name — something like *"Lily's birthday"* — and click **Create**
   (or press Enter). If you leave the name empty, it's called *"Untitled video"*.
3. Your new, empty video opens on the right.

To continue working on a video later, just click its name in the left list. Each row
shows how many clips it has and when it was last changed.

## Adding clips

At the bottom of the editor is a strip called **"Add from your Media Bank"** showing
all your videos and photos (audio clips can't be added here — audio goes in as the
voiceover, see below).

1. Find the clip you want in the strip.
2. Click its **＋ Add** button.
3. It appears in the clip list above, at the end of your video.

Add as many as you like. The **Total** at the top right shows the full length of your
video and updates as you work.

## The clip list

Your video is a simple numbered list — clip 1 plays first, then clip 2, and so on.
Each row shows a thumbnail, the name, and its trim settings.

**Reorder clips:** use the **↑** and **↓** buttons on a row to move it earlier or
later in the video.

**Remove a clip:** click the **✕** button on its row. (This only removes it from the
video — it stays in your Media Bank.)

**Preview a clip:** click the **▶** button. A player opens right under the row and
plays *only the trimmed part* you chose. Click ▶ again to close the preview.

## Trimming videos and audio

For video clips you'll see two small number boxes: **from** *X* **to** *Y* **s** —
the part of the clip that will be used, in seconds.

- Change **from** to skip the beginning (e.g. `2.0` starts two seconds in).
- Change **to** to cut the end off.
- The app keeps things sensible: values can't go below 0, past the clip's length, or
  cross each other, and it shows you the result, e.g. *"(of 1:21 → uses 0:12)"*.

Use the ▶ preview to check your trim — it plays exactly the "from → to" part.

## Setting how long a photo shows

Photos don't have a length of their own, so you choose one. Each photo row says
**"Show photo for ___ seconds"** — type any length from half a second up to
10 minutes. New photos start at **3 seconds** and you can adjust each one separately.

## Adding a voiceover

In the **Voiceover (optional)** box at the bottom:

1. Open the dropdown (it starts on *"No voiceover"*).
2. Pick any audio item from your bank — your recordings and their cleaned versions are
   all listed with their lengths.

Underneath is a checkbox: **"Keep the original sound of the clips"**.

- **Checked (the default):** the finished video keeps the clips' own sound, played
  softly in the background, with your voiceover on top.
- **Unchecked:** the clips' own sound is removed and only your voiceover is heard.

You can hear the difference after [exporting](./05-exporting.md). If you pick no
voiceover, the checkbox simply controls whether clip sound is kept or muted.

## Undo and redo

Made a mistake? The **↩️ Undo** button at the top steps back through your changes —
adding a clip, a trim, a reorder, renaming, anything. **↪️ Redo** puts them back.
You can undo all the way back to when you opened the video.

## Renaming, duplicating and deleting a video

- **Rename:** click into the big title at the top of the editor and type — it saves
  as you go.
- **Duplicate:** in the left list, click the **⧉** button on a video's row. A copy
  named *"... copy"* appears — handy for making a second version.
- **Delete:** click the **🗑** button on a row. The project is removed; the media in
  your bank is not affected.
