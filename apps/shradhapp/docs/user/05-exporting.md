# Exporting your video

Exporting turns your clip list, trims and voiceover into one finished video file you
can watch, keep, or send to family. You export from the **🎬 Make a Video** tab, in
the **Export** box at the bottom right.

## The three choices (no technical knowledge needed)

Pick exactly one of the three buttons:

| Button | What it means | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MP4 — Full quality (1080p)** | A big, beautiful video at TV quality. | Watching on a TV or keeping for the archive. |
| **MP4 — Small (for WhatsApp)** | A smaller file that's much quicker to send. | Sending to family on WhatsApp or by email. |
| **MOV** | The same full quality, in the format some Apple apps ask for. | When another app specifically wants a MOV file. |

If in doubt: choose **MP4 — Full quality** for keeping, **MP4 — Small** for sending.

## Exporting, step by step

1. Make sure your video has at least one clip (the Export button is greyed out until
   it does).
2. Choose your preset — the selected one gets an orange border.
3. (Optional) Set your [voiceover and "keep original sound" choice](./04-making-a-video.md#adding-a-voiceover)
   in the box on the left.
4. Click **💾 Export "your video's name"**.
5. A window opens asking **where to save the file**. Pick a folder — the Desktop or
   your Videos folder are good choices — and confirm. If you change your mind, just
   close the window; nothing is exported.
6. A **progress bar** appears with a percentage and a friendly stage message, such as
   *"Preparing clips"*, *"Joining clips"*, *"Mixing audio"* and *"Finishing up"*.
   Longer videos take longer — this is normal.
7. When it reaches 100%, you'll see **"Finished! Your video was saved to: …"** with
   the exact location. Double-click the file there to watch it.

## Cancelling an export

Changed your mind halfway? Click the red **Cancel** button next to the progress bar.
The export stops, the half-made file isn't kept, and you'll see *"Export cancelled."*

## What the app does for you behind the scenes

You don't need to know this to use the app — but so nothing feels like magic:

- Every clip is trimmed and gently resized to fit the chosen size (with soft borders
  if a photo's shape doesn't fill the frame, so nothing is ever stretched).
- The clips are joined together in your chosen order.
- If you picked a voiceover, it's mixed in — with the clips' own sound kept softly in
  the background when "Keep the original sound" is on, or replaced entirely when off.
- The result is written as a single file where you chose.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Add at least one clip first."** — the video is empty; add clips from the bank.
- **"One of the clips is no longer in the Media Bank…"** — a clip's media was deleted
  from the bank. Remove that row from the clip list and export again.
- **"The folder you chose for the export doesn't exist."** — pick a different folder
  in the save window.
- **An FFmpeg message** — FFmpeg is missing or couldn't be found; see
  [Getting started](./01-getting-started.md#before-you-start-install-ffmpeg).
