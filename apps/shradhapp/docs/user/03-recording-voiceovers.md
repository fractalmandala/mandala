# Recording voiceovers

The **🎙️ Record Voiceover** tab is where you record yourself talking — a narration
for a birthday video, a garden tour, a message to the grandkids. Your recordings are
saved into the Media Bank automatically.

## Recording, step by step

1. Open the **🎙️ Record Voiceover** tab.
2. You'll see one **big red button** with a microphone on it, and the words
   *"Ready when you are"*.
3. **Click the button once** and start talking. The first time you do this, your
   computer may ask whether the app may use the microphone — choose **Allow**.
   (If it's blocked by mistake, see [Troubleshooting](./07-troubleshooting-and-faq.md#the-microphone-is-blocked).)
4. While you record, the button shows a stop symbol, a small red dot pulses, and a
   timer counts up, like `Recording… 0:12`.
5. **Click the button again** when you're done. The app saves the recording right
   away — you'll see *"Saving…"* for a moment.

You don't have to name or file anything. The recording is saved into your
**Media Bank** with a timestamped name (like *"Voiceover 2025-05-04 14.32.webm"*)
and the tag `#voiceover`, so it's easy to find later.

## Listening back

After saving, the page shows **"Your recording"** with a play bar. Press play to
listen. If you don't like it, click **🎙️ Record another** at the bottom and try
again — nothing is lost, and you can always delete old takes from the
[Media Bank](./02-media-bank.md).

## Cleaning up the sound (one click)

Recordings made at home often have a bit of hiss, an uneven volume, and silence at
the start while you get ready. The app can fix all of that for you.

1. After recording, click **✨ Clean up the sound**.
2. Wait a few seconds while it says *"Cleaning up…"*.
3. Done! You'll see a second, green card with the cleaned version and a play bar.

**What "Clean up" actually does, in plain terms:**

- Cuts out low rumble (like a fridge or fan hum).
- Reduces steady background hiss.
- Trims the silence from the start and end of the recording.
- Evens out the loudness so it's not too quiet or too loud.

Because silence is trimmed, the cleaned version is often a little shorter. The app
tells you exactly: **"Before: 0:42 → after: 0:38."**

**Both versions are kept.** The original recording and the cleaned one
(named with *"(cleaned)"* at the end) both live in your Media Bank, both tagged
`#voiceover`. Cleaning up never overwrites anything — you can always go back.

## Using your voiceover in a video

When you're assembling a video, the voiceover picker lists every audio item in your
bank — recordings and cleaned versions alike. See
[Making a video](./04-making-a-video.md#adding-a-voiceover) and
[Exporting](./05-exporting.md) for how the voiceover is mixed into the final video.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The microphone is blocked."** — the app isn't allowed to use the mic. Fix it in
  your computer's settings; see [Troubleshooting](./07-troubleshooting-and-faq.md#the-microphone-is-blocked).
- **"Nothing was recorded."** — the recording came out empty; try again and make sure
  you spoke after the timer started.
- **Clean up fails with an FFmpeg message** — FFmpeg isn't installed or can't be
  found; see [Getting started](./01-getting-started.md#before-you-start-install-ffmpeg).
