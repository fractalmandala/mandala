# Managing your projects

Each video you work on is a **project** — the list of clips, their order and trims,
the voiceover choice, and the name. Projects live in the left column of the
**🎬 Make a Video** tab, under **"Your videos"**.

## Projects save themselves

There is no Save button. Every change — adding a clip, a trim, a reorder, a rename —
is stored automatically a moment after you make it. A small *"✓ Saved"* appears near
the top of the editor to confirm. You can close the app at any time and come back
later; everything will be as you left it.

## What each row shows

Every project row in the list shows:

- its **name**,
- how many **clips** it contains (e.g. *"5 clips"*),
- the **date it was last changed**.

The project you're currently working on is highlighted in orange. Rows are ordered
with the most recently changed at the top.

## The five things you can do

1. **Create** — click **＋ New**, type a name, click **Create**. Empty names become
   *"Untitled video"*.
2. **Open** — click the row's name. Your clips, trims and voiceover choice load on
   the right.
3. **Rename** — with the project open, click into the big title at the top of the
   editor and type. It saves as you go.
4. **Duplicate** — click the **⧉** button on a row. An exact copy named
   *"... copy"* appears and opens. Great for trying a variation without touching the
   original — for example, a short WhatsApp version and a full-length keepsake.
5. **Delete** — click the **🗑** button on a row. Only the project is removed; all
   the media in your [Media Bank](./02-media-bank.md) stays put.

## Projects and the Media Bank

Projects don't contain copies of your videos and photos — they *point at* items in
the Media Bank. Two consequences worth knowing:

- You can reuse the same clip in as many projects as you like, at no extra space.
- If you delete something from the Media Bank that a project uses, that project's row
  shows **"⚠️ This media was removed from the bank"**. Remove that row (the ✕ button)
  before [exporting](./05-exporting.md).

## Where projects are stored

Projects are stored inside the app's own data folder on your computer — the same
place as the media bank — not in your Documents folder. There is no "Save as…" for
projects and nothing extra to back up by hand; see
[Where are my files stored?](./07-troubleshooting-and-faq.md#where-are-my-files-stored)
for the exact locations.

## Coming in a future version

More project conveniences are planned, such as reusing an old video's sequence as a
starting point and an export queue. For now, duplicating a project is the way to
start from something existing.
